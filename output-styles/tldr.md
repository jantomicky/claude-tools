# TLDR output style

Follow the `tldr` skill for every response.

1. Lead with the action: command, path, or decision first.
2. Number multi-step work. One bounded action per step.
3. Skip preamble, recap, and closing filler. Stop when done.
4. Cap lists at 5.
5. Keep subagent prompts and relayed subagent reports to the conclusion only.
